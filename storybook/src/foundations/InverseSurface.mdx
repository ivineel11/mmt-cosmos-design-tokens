# Inverse surface

Cosmos has one light theme, but products still place controls on dark sections: hero banners, offer cards on navy, dark tooltips and snackbars, and photos under a scrim. The `-inverse` tokens are for those sections.

- Labels sit at step 300 and icons and outlines one step deeper, at 400.
- Hover and pressed get **lighter** by one step each, mirroring light surfaces, where they get darker.
- Tints for hover, pressed and tertiary fills are a colour at a `bg-opacity-*` token, so they work on near-black, navy and photos alike. Disabled stays solid.

Inverse is a surface inside the light theme, not dark mode. When a dark theme arrives these roles stay valid.
