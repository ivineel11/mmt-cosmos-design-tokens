# Chip

A compact control for filtering, choosing and tagging: a label with an optional secondary line, an optional leading icon **or** image, and a trailing slot that is either a decorative icon or a separate remove button. Figma set `559:2943`. Full spec: [components/chip.md](https://github.com/ivineel11/mmt-cosmos-design-tokens/blob/main/components/chip.md).

## Selection and border

The border is a layer, not a variant: choose it by canvas. Turn it on for white pages and cards, and off on the grey `bg-secondary` canvas, where the white chip already stands off the page. The label stays bold when selected, so a row of chips never jumps.

<Canvas of={Stories.Selection} />

On the grey canvas without a border, a selected chip differs from the others only by its label colour, which is not enough on its own. **A borderless selected filter chip must show a check** in the leading slot.

## Examples

Filters toggle (`aria-pressed`). A date picker is a single choice: a `radiogroup` of radio chips with arrow-key navigation. A removable chip has two focus stops, the chip and its `Remove {label}` button.

## Accessibility

- `selectionRole` picks the semantics: `toggle` for filters, `radio` inside a `role="radiogroup"` for single choice, `none` for an action or a menu trigger (add `aria-haspopup`).
- The secondary text joins the accessible name after the label. Icons are hidden; the leading image is decorative (`alt=""`).
- The hit area reaches `chip/min-touch-target` (48) without changing the drawn chip, and the remove button reaches 24.
- `disabled` wins: a disabled chip is not selectable and its remove button is inert.
