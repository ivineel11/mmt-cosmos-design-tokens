# Segmented control

Switches between two to five options in place, on mobile, such as One way / Round trip / Multi-city. Exactly one segment is selected and the change applies straight away. Figma sets `765:186` and `764:131`. Full spec: [components/segmented-control.md](https://github.com/ivineel11/mmt-cosmos-design-tokens/blob/main/components/segmented-control.md).

## Tap and slide

Tap a segment and the thumb slides to it. Or press the thumb and drag it: it follows the pointer, the label under it turns to its selected colour, and on release it snaps to the nearest enabled segment. `onChange` fires once, on release. A mostly vertical drag cancels, so the page can still scroll. The thumb scales to 0.95 while pressed.

## Accessibility

- `role="radiogroup"` named by `aria-label`, with `role="radio"` segments. Arrow keys move and select.
- Dragging is never the only way: keyboard and screen reader users select segments directly.
- The focus ring sits outside the segment, on the track, so it shows on every thumb style.
- **Known gap:** the neutral white thumb is 1.09:1 against the track; its shadow and the brand label carry the selection.
