# Slider

Picks a value, or a range, by dragging a white, raised thumb along a track: price per night, departure time, distance from the centre. Pass a number for a single slider or a `[min, max]` pair for a range. Figma set `811:1622`. Full spec: [components/slider.md](https://github.com/ivineel11/mmt-cosmos-design-tokens/blob/main/components/slider.md).

## Types

Continuous moves freely; Discrete snaps to `step` and can show a tick at each step (12 steps or fewer). Range thumbs never cross and stay `minGap` apart. Hover, press and focus a thumb to see its states; the tooltip shows while a thumb is held or keyboard-focused.

## Accessibility

- Each thumb is a `role="slider"` with `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-valuenow` and `aria-valuetext` from `formatValue`. Range thumbs are named "Minimum …" and "Maximum …".
- Keyboard: arrows move one step (1% when continuous), Page Up and Page Down move 10%, Home and End go to the ends.
- The header value, limits and tooltip are hidden from assistive tech; the slider value already says it.
- Each thumb takes pointer input in a 48 × 48 area (24 × 24 on Small).
- **Known gap:** the white thumb is 1.48:1 on the inactive track; the shadow carries its edge.
